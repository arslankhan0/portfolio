Control System Development of Dual Motor Control for RCWS

Overview

Design and development of a dual-motor, closed-loop control system for coordinated pan–tilt actuation in a remote controlled weapon station (RCWS) platform. High-torque servo/BLDC motors with precision encoders were driven through a coordinated dual-axis control scheme, with embedded firmware handling motor control, safety interlocks, and synchronized multi-axis motion. The work focused on reliable, repeatable control performance under varying load and field conditions.
