Autonomous Target Tracking via Drone

Overview

An autonomous drone-based tracking system for following moving targets in real time. Vision-based detection and tracking (YOLO / OpenCV) were integrated with GPS and IMU data for stable flight control. The system includes obstacle-aware autonomous navigation and live video streaming, evaluated for surveillance and monitoring applications.
