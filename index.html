About

Working at the hardware–software boundary

Across a bachelor's thesis on visual-inertial state estimation for UAVs, a research internship, an engineering role in industry, and a long-running set of independent projects, I've studied and built systems spanning embedded firmware, control loops, sensor integration, computer vision, and printed-circuit design.
