Autonomous UGV with Robotic Arm

Overview

A study in autonomous ground-vehicle navigation combined with robotic manipulation. SLAM-based mapping and obstacle avoidance were implemented alongside multi-sensor fusion (LiDAR, camera, IMU) within ROS for real-time decision-making and path planning. A robotic arm was programmed for precise manipulation, allowing the platform to detect, grasp, and place objects in dynamic environments.
