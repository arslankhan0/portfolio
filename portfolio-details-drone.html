Custom Drone and Flight Controller Development

Overview

An applied study in flight-controller design for autonomous and manually piloted multirotor platforms. IMU, GPS, and barometric sensor data were fused within a real-time control loop to achieve stable flight and obstacle-aware navigation. The resulting modular architecture supported FPV racing configurations, payload deployment, and further autonomy experiments within ROS.
